# Go-To-Market

## Beachhead
**US TikTok Shop new sellers with ~$3,000 first budget.** Narrow on purpose:
one platform, one country, one budget band, one painful job (don't blow the
test budget on a bad first order).

## Positioning
"Atlaz runs your first cross-border trade end-to-end so you don't lose your
$3,000 on the wrong supplier or a banned listing."

Not "find products" (crowded). Instead "execute the trade and remember it."

## Sequenced motion
1. **Phase 1 — Hand-recruited beta (PLG seed).** 20–50 sellers from TikTok Shop
   seller communities. Watch the must-pass story end to end; instrument the
   first *real decision* as activation.
2. **Phase 2 — Public PLG launch.** Free tier + the "first win in one session"
   loop. SEO compounding content (per region/platform/category compliance
   pages). Dogfood short-video distribution.
3. **Phase 3 — Two-sided network.** Verified suppliers invite their merchants;
   merchants invite trusted suppliers. Compliance/freight/lab partners.

## Channels (ranked by CAC efficiency)
1. Product-led organic + referral (lowest CAC).
2. SEO content backed by real Compliance Routes.
3. Short-video native (the tool advertising itself).
4. Community wedges: TikTok Shop groups, Shopify app store, FBA forums, reseller chats.
5. Supplier-side flywheel.
6. Paid only to amplify what already converts organically.

## Messaging by persona
| Persona | Hook |
|---|---|
| US TikTok new seller | "Don't lose your $3k on a bad first order." |
| UK Shopify pet seller | "Pick a supplier you can actually trust." |
| DE/FR Amazon seller | "Pass compliance before you list, not after a takedown." |

## Metrics that matter
Activation (first real decision) > signups. Net revenue retention > new logos.
Time-to-first-Deal-Room-decision is the north-star input metric.

## Expansion path
US TikTok → UK Shopify → DE/FR Amazon → SEA/ME/LatAm. Each new market reuses the
Compliance Memory and Supplier Trust Graph already built — expansion gets cheaper.
