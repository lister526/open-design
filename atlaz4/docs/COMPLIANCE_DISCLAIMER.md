# Compliance Disclaimer

**This disclaimer is shown on every compliance result in the product.**

> The compliance output Atlaz produces is a **general operational risk signal
> only**. It is **not legal advice** and does **not** guarantee that a product
> is permitted, certified, or safe to sell in any market or on any platform.
>
> Before selling, you must independently verify requirements with **qualified
> compliance professionals, accredited testing laboratories, the platform's own
> rules, licensed customs brokers, and/or local legal counsel**.
>
> Regulations, banned-keyword lists, certification requirements, and platform
> policies change frequently and vary by country, category, and channel. Atlaz's
> mock data is illustrative and may be incomplete or out of date.
>
> **You, the seller, are solely responsible** for the legality, safety,
> labeling, and compliance of anything you import, list, or sell.

## High-risk handling
- High-risk categories (e.g. baby, ingestibles, cosmetics with health claims,
  mains-powered electronics, lithium batteries) are flagged in **red** with a
  `no_go_until_certified` recommendation.
- Generating a compliance statement is a **high-risk action requiring human
  approval**; Atlaz never auto-issues compliance claims.

## What Atlaz never does
- Never claims a product is "FDA-approved", "certified", or "safe" on your behalf.
- Never tells you to make medical, curative, or unverifiable claims.
- Never substitutes for a lawyer, lab, or customs broker.
