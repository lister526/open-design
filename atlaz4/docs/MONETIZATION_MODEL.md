# Monetization Model — Including Distribution & High-Profit Techniques

> This document covers (a) how Atlaz makes money, (b) **distribution / 发行**
> techniques to acquire users cheaply, and (c) **high-profit / 高额盈利**
> techniques that raise margin and lifetime value. All figures are planning
> assumptions, not guarantees, and Atlaz is not yet a registered business
> (see `LEGAL_LIMITATIONS.md`).

---

## 1. Why Atlaz can charge real money

Atlaz is not a "tool" the user could cancel and forget. The longer a merchant
uses it, the more their **SKU Passports, Supplier Trust Graph, and Compliance
Memory** accumulate — switching cost grows with usage. That is the structural
basis for durable, high-margin revenue.

## 3. Distribution techniques (发行)

The cheapest customer is one who arrives because of the product itself.

1. **Product-led "first win" loop.** New seller completes the must-pass story
   (opportunity → supplier → cash check → listing → test → decision) in one
   session. The first *real decision* is the activation event — instrument it.
2. **SEO compounding content.** Auto-generate (with human review) long-tail
   pages: "How to import a [category] to [country] on [platform]". Each maps to
   a real Compliance Route Atlaz already computes — content that no generic blog
   can match because it's backed by the product's own structured data.
3. **Short-video native distribution.** Atlaz literally produces TikTok video
   scripts and creator briefs — dogfood it. Post the *process* ("watch a $3k
   seller pick a winning product in 20 minutes"). The tool is its own ad.
4. **Supplier-side flywheel.** Verified suppliers invite the merchants they
   already serve, and merchants invite suppliers they trust. Two-sided referral.
5. **Marketplace / community wedges.** Distribute through TikTok Shop seller
   groups, Shopify app store, Amazon FBA communities, and reseller Telegram/
   WhatsApp groups where the ICP already gathers.
6. **Templated SKU Passports as lead magnets.** Publish free, partial SKU
   Passports for trending products; unlock the full Deal Room behind signup.
7. **Referral with asymmetric reward.** Give the *referrer* AI credits and the
   *referee* a free Growth-tier month — credits cost you little, retention is high.

## 4. High-profit techniques (高额盈利)

Raising margin and lifetime value, not just price.

1. **Sell the outcome, not the seat.** Frame Pro around "protect your $3,000
   test budget and avoid one bad order" — one avoided mistake pays for a year.
2. **Annual + usage hybrid.** Annual subscription stabilizes cash; metered AI
   credits capture upside from power users without raising base price.
3. **Take-rate on executed trades (the big one).** When real payment/escrow
   adapters ship, a 1–2% fee on facilitated trade value can exceed all
   subscription revenue combined, at very high margin, because the product
   already orchestrates the deal.
4. **Supplier-paid placement.** Suppliers pay for verified placement and lead
   flow. Merchants never pay for it, so it is incremental, near-100%-margin
   revenue layered on top of subscriptions.
5. **Compliance/lab/freight affiliate fees** captured at the exact moment of
   need (the Deal Room already knows the SKU needs a CE test) — extremely high
   conversion, high margin.
6. **Expansion revenue > acquisition.** Add seats, Deal Rooms, currencies, and
   API access as the merchant grows. Net revenue retention above 110% is the
   real profit engine — existing customers paying more each year.
7. **Cost discipline on AI.** The 15-field envelope + Tool Adapter layer let you
   route cheap models for routine drafts and expensive models only for
   high-stakes work-orders, protecting gross margin as you scale.
8. **Cohort-based pricing experiments.** Because every action is logged in the
   Trade Loop Ledger, you can measure willingness-to-pay by behavior and price
   to value, not to cost.
