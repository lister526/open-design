# Atlaz — Privacy Notes

## Principles (consent-first, merchant-owned)
- Merchants **own** their SKU Passports, trade data and Trade Loop Ledger.
- Cross-merchant insights are derived **only from anonymized, consented** signals.
- **No end-customer personal data** is collected in the MVP.
- Suppliers control whether their trust signals enter the graph.
- All data sharing is **purpose-bound and revocable**.
- Atlaz **does not sell data** to third parties; only aggregated, consented insights.

## Data Boundaries (MVP)
- Data is stored locally / in the merchant's workspace (mock) — no real backend in the prototype.
- The Trade Loop Ledger is **append-only** and owned by the merchant.
- Tool integrations transmit **only what the user approves** (Human-approved Agent Actions).
- Compliance and finance outputs are **estimates, never guarantees**.

## Disclaimers
This prototype uses **mock data only**. No real orders, messages, payments or certifications are
created. See `COMPLIANCE_DISCLAIMER.md`, `DATA_RIGHTS_AND_PRIVACY_BOUNDARIES.md`, `OWNERSHIP_NOTICE.md`.
