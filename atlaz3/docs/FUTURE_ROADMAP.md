# Atlaz — Future Roadmap (4 Phases)

## Phase 1 — Single-SKU Trade Loop (this MVP)
- iOS-first app; 5 tabs; AI Trade Command Center; 28 pages.
- 6 moat objects with mock data; 17 AI methods; 15 reserved tools.
- Complete loop: opportunity → passport → suppliers → compliance → cash → listing → order → growth → decision.
- **Outcome:** a merchant can run one full, de-risked trade loop and export a SKU Passport.

## Phase 2 — Real Integrations & Multi-SKU
- Swap mock tools for production APIs (sourcing, FX, logistics, listing, customs) — see `API_INTEGRATION_ROADMAP.md`.
- Multi-SKU portfolio; cross-SKU cash and inventory view.
- Real (opt-in) trade signals begin populating the Supplier Trust Graph.
- **Outcome:** Atlaz becomes the merchant's daily operating tool, not a one-off.

## Phase 3 — Factory Portal & Trust Graph at Scale
- `FACTORY_PORTAL_ROADMAP.md`: suppliers onboard, claim profiles, contribute verified signals.
- Two-sided network effects; supplier verification revenue.
- Benchmarks and market signals from anonymized, consented data across many merchants.
- **Outcome:** the Supplier Trust Graph becomes the most trusted source of small-trade supplier truth.

## Phase 4 — Trade-Finance & Marketplace Transaction Layer
- Trade-finance referrals / inventory finance partners (consent + eligibility, never guarantees).
- Marketplace take-rate as transactions flow through Atlaz.
- Atlaz as the **system of record and operating network** for small global trade.
- **Outcome:** a 12–15-year network company at the center of small-merchant cross-border commerce.

> Each phase deepens the moats: more loops → richer SKU Passports, longer ledgers, stronger
> trust graph, sharper compliance/cash intelligence, and safer agent automation.
