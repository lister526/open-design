# Atlaz — Go-To-Market

## Wedge
Land merchants at the single most painful, highest-stakes moment: **"I found a product — should
I actually order it?"** Atlaz answers with a compliant listing, a supplier match, and a Cash
Conversion Score that says buy/negotiate/test/stop. That single moment of clarity is the hook.

## Channels (creator-led, content-first)
1. **Sell where they sell.** Short-form demos on TikTok showing "I ran this product through
   Atlaz and here's the buy/stop decision" — the same format our users use to sell.
2. **Shareable SKU Passports.** Exported SKU Passport JSON / cards are inherently viral artifacts
   sellers post and compare.
3. **Compliance fear → relief content.** "Don't get your listing banned — here's the compliance route."
4. **Supplier-side seeding** (future Factory Portal): verified suppliers invite their buyers.
5. **Communities & agencies.** Seller Discords, Amazon/Shopify groups, sourcing agents.

## Funnel
Content → Free app (skip-friendly onboarding) → **first completed Trade Loop (activation)** →
**second SKU Passport (retention)** → Pro upgrade → add-ons → Enterprise/seats.

## Pricing-led growth
Free is genuinely useful (1 loop). Pro unlocks the moat engines (Compliance Route, Cash
Conversion, Agent Actions, unlimited passports). See `MONETIZATION_MODEL.md`.

## Metrics
North star = **completed Trade Loops per merchant per month.** Track activation, second-SKU
retention, loops/merchant, and moat-data accumulation (ledger events, trust signals).
