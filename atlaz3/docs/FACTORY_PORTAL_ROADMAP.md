# Atlaz — Factory Portal Roadmap

The Factory Portal turns Atlaz from a merchant tool into a **two-sided network** (Phase 3).

## Vision
Suppliers onboard, claim and verify their profiles, respond to RFQs, and contribute verified
trade signals — directly strengthening the Supplier Trust Graph that merchants rely on.

## Phased rollout
1. **Claim & verify (early Phase 3):** suppliers claim a mock profile, submit verification
   evidence (business license, certs, sample history). Status moves Unverified → Partially → Verified.
2. **RFQ inbox:** suppliers receive Atlaz RFQs, send quotes, and (with consent) feed quote/lead-time
   data back into the graph.
3. **Signal contribution:** on-time delivery, dispute resolution, sample accuracy become first-class,
   consented signals — replacing mock signals with real ones.
4. **Reputation incentives:** higher trust → more merchant matches → revenue motivation to perform.

## Economics
- **Supplier verification fees** (revenue stream #3).
- Better matches reduce merchant risk → more loops → more demand for suppliers.

## Guardrails
- Suppliers control their data (`DATA_RIGHTS_AND_PRIVACY_BOUNDARIES.md`).
- Trust signals are evidence-based and auditable via the ledger.
- No pay-to-win trust: scores reflect performance, not payment.

## Dependencies
Requires Phase 2 real integrations (inspection, certification verification, payment) and the
mature Trade Graph (`TRADE_GRAPH_SPEC.md`, `SUPPLIER_TRUST_SCORE.md`).
