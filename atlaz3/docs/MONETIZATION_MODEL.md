# Atlaz — Monetization Model

## Plans
| Plan | Price | For | Highlights |
|------|-------|-----|-----------|
| **Free** | $0 | First-time sellers | 1 active SKU Passport, core AI drafts, mock tools, 1 trade loop |
| **Pro** | $29/mo | Active sellers | Unlimited SKU Passports, Compliance Route Engine, Cash Conversion Score, Agent Actions |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Teams/agencies | Seats, API access, priority support, custom data rights |

## 12 Revenue Streams
1. **Pro subscription** (core).
2. **Enterprise seats.**
3. **Supplier verification fees** (verify a factory into the Trust Graph).
4. **Trade document / export add-ons.**
5. **Compliance route premium** (deeper, market-specific routes).
6. **Logistics rate referrals** (freight forwarders).
7. **Payment / FX referrals.**
8. **Ad-platform integration tier** (publish & manage tests).
9. **Listing publishing add-on** (push to store platforms).
10. **Trade-finance referrals** (future; BNPL / inventory finance partners).
11. **Data insights** (anonymized, consented benchmarks).
12. **Marketplace take-rate** (future; transaction layer).

## Sequencing
- **Near-term (MVP/Phase 1–2):** subscription + document/compliance add-ons + verification fees.
- **Mid-term (Phase 3):** integration tiers, ad/listing publishing, logistics & payment referrals.
- **Long-term (Phase 4):** trade-finance referrals and marketplace take-rate as Atlaz becomes a
  transaction layer.

## Why it compounds
Moat data lowers AI/tooling cost per loop and raises conversion, so gross margin and LTV improve
as the network grows. **No loan, profit or sales guarantees are made to users.**
