## The two hard truths these reveal
1. **The winning MODEL is proven**: outcome-priced, workflow-embedded AI that replaces human
   labor the customer ALREADY pays for (Decagon per-resolution; Harvey per-lawyer-seat). [E-shape]
2. **The winning CATEGORIES in the West are already contested by elite, well-capitalized,
   technical teams.** A China non-technical founder cloning Harvey/Sierra/Decagon = roadkill. [I]
   → Our edge must be a wedge where WE have asymmetry they don't.

## Where does a China + non-technical + AI-leveraged founder have ASYMMETRIC advantage?
Asymmetry sources available to THIS founder:
  (i) Native access to China's supply base / manufacturers / cross-border sellers (relationship + language + trust + proximity). 
  (ii) China's cost & speed advantage in content/ops/human-in-the-loop delivery.
  (iii) Cultural/linguistic bridge BETWEEN China supply and global demand.
  (iv) Willingness to do service-first, human+AI, in markets Western VCs find "too operational."
Asymmetry we LACK: deep-tech, regulated-vertical trust (health/legal), enterprise sales in the West,
big capital. → Reject wedges that depend on what we lack.
