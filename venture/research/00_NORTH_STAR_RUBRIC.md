# 北极星评估准星 · The $100B Filter
*(Durable spec. Every candidate wedge is scored against THIS, not against vibes.)*
Last updated: 2026-08-16

## 0. Mission restatement (the 准星)
We are NOT hunting a project that nets tens of millions RMB.
We are running a global carpet-bomb evidence hunt to find the ONE wedge that can compound
into a **$100B+ (ideally $1T-capable) company**, then build a mature, production-grade
multi-platform product (Web + iOS + Android + WeChat Mini Program) with real ad-network
integration (AppLovin / Meta / etc.).

A wedge only advances if it plausibly clears ALL five structural pre-conditions of giants.
Missing even one caps the outcome far below $100B — so a missing pillar is a HARD reject
for the mission (though it may still be a fine small business, which is not our goal).

---

## 1. The 5 structural pre-conditions of $100B companies (HARD gates)
Historically, near-every company that reached ~$100B+ had ALL of these. We treat them as gates.

| # | Pillar | Test question | Hard-fail if... |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Global, expanding demand** | Is the need present & growing across ≥4 major regions, not one country/policy window? | Demand is single-country, or shrinking, or a fad |
| G2 | **Structural, recurring pain** | Does the pain recur at high frequency AND is the customer already paying real money to fix it? | One-time need, or nobody currently pays |
| G3 | **Compounding proprietary data / network moat** | Does every unit of usage make the product measurably harder to copy (data, graph, network, switching cost)? | Value = prompt+UI; a frontier LLM feature erases it |
| G4 | **Non-substitutability by frontier models** | Can OpenAI/Google/Anthropic NOT casually absorb this with one feature? Is there proprietary access/distribution/regulatory/data they lack? | GPT/Gemini can do it free tomorrow |
| G5 | **Durable, high-margin, scalable economics** | Gross margin can reach ≥60% at scale; unit economics improve with volume; not headcount-linear | Margin stays agency-like; cost scales with revenue |

**Rule:** A wedge that fails any Gate is logged but NOT shortlisted for the mission.
It may be noted as a "cash bridge" option separately.

## 2. 100-point score (only applied to wedges that PASS all 5 gates)
Used to rank the survivors. Weighted toward what makes giants, not what makes revenue fast.

| Dimension | Weight | What we look for |
|---|---|---|
| Pain intensity / willingness-to-pay | 16 | Users already spend a lot AND hate current solution |
| Frequency / retention / habit | 14 | Weekly+ usage; consumed repeatedly; embeds in workflow |
| Data/network moat compounding rate | 16 | How fast does each use widen the gap? |
| Non-substitutability (vs frontier AI & incumbents) | 12 | Proprietary access others structurally can't get |
| Global TAM & expansion path | 12 | Bottom-up TAM ≥ tens of $B; clean multi-region rollout |
| Distribution advantage / wedge | 8 | Where clients already aggregate; not paid-UA-dependent |
| Founder fit (non-tech, China, humanities, AI-leveraged) | 8 | Can start selling/validating without world-class eng |
| 0→revenue speed & capital efficiency | 8 | Validatable with little capital, service-first OK |
| Regulatory / data-legality / platform risk | 6 | Legally acquirable data; not one-platform-dependent |

Total 100. Penalties (subtract): generic AI wrapper (−15), single-platform dependency (−10),
paid-UA-growth dependency (−10), two-sided cold-start (−10), needs licenses/PhD/relationships
on day 1 (−10), heavy capital to even validate (−10).

## 3. Evidence discipline (non-negotiable)
Every material claim tagged:
- `[E]` verified evidence (I fetched a real source; cite URL + date)
- `[M]` media/single-source (needs cross-check)
- `[F]` founder/vendor self-reported
- `[3P]` third-party estimate (getlatka-type; treat with suspicion)
- `[I]` my inference
- `[A]` assumption to be validated
- `[U]` unverifiable

Hinge numbers (anything a $100B decision rests on) require ≥2 independent sources before
they move from `[M]` to `[E]`. No invented user quotes. Ever.

## 4. Research coverage requirements (carpet-bomb, not cherry-pick)
- **Sectors:** AI-native services, vertical AI, China-global friction, global B2B back-office,
  data/intelligence, consumer/creator, health/bio-adjacent-software, fintech/payments,
  climate/energy-software, dev/AI-infrastructure, marketplaces/transaction, robotics-software,
  education, security/compliance. (≥30 specific wedges, not categories.)
- **Regions:** China, US, Canada, UK, EU (DE/FR), Japan, Korea, SEA, India, Middle East (GCC),
  LatAm (BR/MX), Australia. Compare CAC × ACV × regulation × payment ability × China-founder edge.
- **User-voice sources:** Reddit, G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, App/Play Store reviews, X,
  YouTube comments, Hacker News, GitHub issues, LinkedIn, industry forums, 小红书, 知乎, 抖音,
  B站, 微信公开, hiring JDs, procurement/RFP portals, earnings calls, company pricing pages.
- **Failure libraries:** CB Insights post-mortems, GetAutopsy, Startup Graveyard, Failory.

---

## 5. Output cadence per research turn
Each turn appends to /venture/research/ with:
1. What was searched (queries + sources actually fetched)
2. Findings, evidence-tagged
3. Which gates each candidate passes/fails
4. Updated shortlist + scores
5. Open questions / next verifications
No product code until a wedge passes all 5 gates AND wins the score AND survives a short-thesis.
