### Q2: What actually is a moat at the AI app layer? (to sharpen Gate G3)
Searched: a16z / NFX / sequoia defensibility essays
Findings:
- a16z "The Empty Promise of Data Moats" (2019) + V7 "Are Data Moats Dead" (2025): raw/generic
  data is NOT a moat. [E]
- NFX "AI defensibility": network effects (each user improves product for others) + embedding. [E]
- Consensus emerging [E/M]: moat = **proprietary INTERACTION / OUTCOME data** + network effects +
  workflow lock-in + switching cost + trust. Not "we have data."
IMPLICATION for our rubric [I]: G3 must require *proprietary interaction/outcome data others
structurally cannot obtain* — e.g. because we sit in a workflow, a transaction, or a
relationship the incumbents/LLM labs don't have access to. Pure scraping fails G3.
